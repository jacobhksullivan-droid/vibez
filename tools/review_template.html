Coast Call · NSW 21–31 Oct 2026 · draft v2

Spot review

Every spot the app will score, Merimbula to Coffs. Check the pins, which way each spot faces, and what it's sheltered from. Mark each one OK or "needs fix", then tap Copy my notes and paste them into the chat.

Rules the app applies

  • A spot gets a "no spearing" warning only if its pin is inside a sanctuary zone or sanctuary aquatic reserve.
  • Spear spots are all headlands, points or rocky bays, because spearing is banned on ocean beaches.

Where the local knowledge came from

  • Surf: every Wannasurf break from Merimbula to Coffs (439 pages of surfer-written guides: swell and wind directions, tides, level, dangers, notes).
  • Spear: Michael McFadyen's NSW shore-dive guides, Dive Swansea, Fish Rock and SWR dive centres, Dive Forster, USFA (the spearfishing association) submissions, spearo trip reports, Deckee/Fishraider threads, local fishing reports and the official DPI fishing guides.
  • What changed: spots that kept getting praised came in (e.g. Kiama Blowhole Point, Penguin Head, Tathra Wharf, Merimbula Wharf, Swansea Heads, Boat Harbour, Ladies Reef, Durras, Potato Point, McKenzies, Pambula Beach); spots with no support or a poor reputation went out (Snapper Point, Broulee, Short Point). Rules like "any E/NE swell crashes into the Skillion entry" are now local rules the scoring uses.
  • Round 3 (Google + Reddit via your Chrome): Reddit itself is blocked, so I read Reddit, forum and Facebook-group snippets through Google (and checked Google's AI answers, which are mostly generic). New: Maloneys Bay (Bass Point), Redhead Point, Hat Head, Seven Mile Beach (Gerroa), Cuttagee, Blacksmiths, Boomerang Beach, Scotts Head, Arrawarra Point. Locals' tips added to Currarong, Bermagui, Swansea, Boat Harbour, Seal Rocks, Trial Bay, The Farm, Shelly, Soldiers, Nobbys, Diggers.
  • Couldn't read: full Reddit threads, Deckee's map and spot reviews (login), DeeperBlue forum (guest limit). Paste threads or screenshots and I'll add them.
